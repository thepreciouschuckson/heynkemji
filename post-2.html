Picking up writing again

Why this journal exists, what it's for, and why it isn't going to stay on one topic.

3 min read Life Meta

I almost made this a product blog. It would have been the easy choice — I have the most material there, and it's the part of my week that feels most like "content." But that's not really why I wanted a journal in the first place.

I wanted somewhere to write regularly again, without deciding in advance what it has to be about. Some weeks that'll be product work. Some weeks it'll be a freelance project that went sideways, or something about school, or nothing to do with work at all.

The point isn't the topic. The point is keeping the habit.

So this is the actual starting entry, even though it isn't the first one published. Everything from here just gets written as it comes, and posted when it's ready — not on a schedule, and not on a beat.
